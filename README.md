# 🛒 Retail Sales Data Analysis

**Author:** Gagan Kumar  
**Date:** April 29, 2025  

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes a retail transaction dataset to uncover insights into customer spending behavior and evaluate the effectiveness of marketing responses. The analysis includes SQL querying, data preprocessing in Python, and a presentation of key findings.

---

## 🧾 Files Included

| File | Description |
|------|-------------|
| `retail_sql.sql` | SQL queries used for extracting and analyzing data from the database. |
| `retailistudio.ipynb` | Python notebook for data cleaning, visualization, and advanced insights. |
| `Sales_Analysis_Presentation.pptx` | Final PowerPoint presentation summarizing the analysis and recommendations. |

---

## 🎯 Objective

- Understand customer behavior through transaction data.
- Measure marketing campaign effectiveness.
- Provide actionable business recommendations.

---

## 📊 Key Insights

- **Total responses:** 6,884  
- **Average Transaction Value:** ₹70.00  
- **Responders:** 647 (≈9.4%)  
- **Non-responders:** 6,237 (≈90.6%)  
- **Top 10 Customers Identified by Total Spend**  
- **Monthly Sales Trends:** Aggregated revenue and transaction counts show seasonality.

---

## 🔍 Additional Insights

### 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Customer Behavior Analysis

- **Customer Segmentation:**  
  Customers were grouped by total spend and marketing response to identify high-value, high-engagement individuals.

- **Repeat Purchases:**  
  A significant share of transactions came from returning customers, suggesting strong customer loyalty.

- **Top 10 Customers:**  
  These customers contributed a substantial portion of total revenue, showing the importance of VIP retention.

---

### 📈 Monthly Trends & Seasonality

- **Sales Peaks:**  
  Highest transaction volumes were observed in festive months, indicating strong seasonal buying behavior.

- **Low-Performance Months:**  
  Some months consistently underperformed and can be boosted using strategic offers or discounts.

---

### 📬 Marketing Response Patterns

- **Monthly Response Rate:**  
  SQL calculates monthly responder ratios to track campaign effectiveness over time.

- **Customer Value Comparison:**  
  Responders had a higher average transaction value than non-responders.

- **Potential for Targeting:**  
  With demographic integration, campaigns can be better tailored to high-response customer profiles.

---

### 📉 Churn Risk Identification

- **Declining Frequency:**  
  Users with a drop in purchase frequency over time were marked as potential churn risks.

- **Re-engagement Plan:**  
  These customers can be targeted with personalized reactivation campaigns.

---

## 🗃️ Data Sources

- `retail_transactions`: Contains `customer_id`, `trans_date`, and `tran_amount`.
- `retail_response`: Contains `customer_id` and `response` (0 or 1) for marketing campaign participation.

## 🧹 Tools & Techniques

- **SQL:** Data extraction, filtering, and joins
- **Python (Pandas, Matplotlib):** Data cleaning, analysis, and visualization
- **PowerPoint:** Business storytelling and presentation of insights

---

## ✅ Recommendations

- Focus marketing efforts on top customers.
- Retarget previous responders with personalized offers.
- Schedule promotions during peak months and boost visibility during off-peak months.
- Identify and re-engage churn-risk customers to improve retention.

---

## 📥 How to Use

1. Run `retail_sql.sql` on your SQL-compatible database to extract and analyze data.
2. Open `retailistudio.ipynb` in Jupyter or VS Code for Python-based data analysis.
3. Review `Sales_Analysis_Presentation.pptx` for a business-ready summary of findings and recommendations.
